Scattering physics

The two processes that compete at low recoil energy: neutron-nucleus elastic scattering and neutron-electron magnetic scattering.

Folder: 1-Physical-Processes/. The notebooks build up the cross sections, first for the neutron-nucleus process (the competing background), then for the neutron-electron process.

A. Neutron-nucleus differential cross sections

Extracts the differential elastic scattering cross sections of the silicon and germanium isotopes from the ENDF/B-VIII.0 evaluated nuclear data files. These are the center-of-mass angular distributions that the next steps turn into recoil spectra.

B. The neutron-electron magnetic process

Single-scatter recoil spectrum of an electron struck by a neutron. In the center-of-mass frame the angular distribution is isotropic, so for a monoenergetic neutron the electron recoil spectrum is flat from 0 up to $E_r^{max} = 4m_em_n E_n/(m_e+m_n)^2$. The notebook computes the total magnetic cross section $\sigma = 8\pi m_n^2\gamma_n^2\mu_B^2/\hbar^2$ (in barns) and the differential cross section in recoil energy.
