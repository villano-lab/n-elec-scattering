A. Matching our flux estimate to the literature

Scales the isotropic flux estimate of the previous section, taken to be the flux in the SNOLAB ladder lab, to the shielded environments of published dark matter searches, and compares it with the simulated estimates in those papers. This tests the assumptions of a common flux shape, single scatters only, the surrounding material composition and self-shielding.
