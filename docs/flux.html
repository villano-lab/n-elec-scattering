Contamination

A. Where the contamination numbers come from

Explains how the uranium and thorium contamination fed to SOURCES4A is obtained and why. 1 ppb is a mass fraction (1 ng/g) converted to atoms/cm³; the rest of each decay chain follows from half-lives and branching ratios under secular equilibrium; the shotcrete and norite compositions give the target atoms. The notebook reproduces the numbers in the workbook decay_chains_data.xlsx.

SOURCES4A

B. Running SOURCES4A

SOURCES4A (with the Sheffield modifications) takes the contamination of a material and returns the neutron production per volume per second from (α,n) reactions and spontaneous fission. The notebook shows the input for 1 ppb of the Th-232 chain, with its three stanzas: material composition, contaminant isotopes and their atom densities, and the (α,n) targets.

Flux in the cavern

C. Slab source and shielding

Combines the neutron production rate of the shotcrete with basic neutron transport to estimate the ambient flux in neutrons/cm²/s, adapting rectangular volume-source formulas from the gamma literature. The shielding by the norite behind the shotcrete is treated the same way, assuming a cavern made of norite of orthopyroxene and calcium-rich plagioclase.

Recoil spectra

D. Flux convolution

Replaces the fixed neutron energies of the scattering-physics notebooks by the realistic flux spectrum, so that many neutron energies contribute to each recoil energy, and computes the predicted nuclear recoil spectrum.
